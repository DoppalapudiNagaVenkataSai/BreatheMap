About BreatheMap

BreatheMap helps urban residents reduce how much air pollution they personally breathe in by deciding when to go out, how to travel, and which route to take.

1. Personal Exposure vs AQI

An AQI number alone does not reflect personal intake. Your inhaled pollution depends on the route chosen, trip duration, breathing rate, microenvironment (in-vehicle vs pedestrian), and departure timing.

2. Inhaled Dose Formula

The comparative PM2.5 dose (µg) is modeled across route segments:

Dose = Σ C_i(t_i) × F_mode × F_road,i × V_mode × Δt_i

Where V_mode is ventilation rate, F_mode is transit microenvironment filtering, and F_road is proximity to heavy traffic corridors.

3. Indicative CPCB NAQI

In India, BreatheMap defaults to an indicative National Air Quality Index (NAQI) calculated from Open-Meteo modelled pollutant data. It is not an official CPCB monitoring station reading.

4. Privacy and AI Transparency

BreatheMap does not require user accounts or store personal identities. When using the AI Assistant, factual summaries are processed using Google Gemini on the free tier. Please do not share sensitive personal information.

5. Not Medical Advice

BreatheMap is a comparative decision tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or guarantee safety.
